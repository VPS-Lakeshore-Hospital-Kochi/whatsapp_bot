# Leave policy (SAMPLE — fake values for development only)

## Casual leave
Permanent employees get 12 days of casual leave per calendar year. Casual leave cannot be
carried forward to the next year. Apply at least one day in advance through the HRMS.

## Sick leave
Sick leave of more than two consecutive days needs a medical certificate uploaded to the HRMS.
