# Visiting hours (SAMPLE — fake values for development only)

## General wards
Visiting hours for general wards are 4:00 pm to 7:00 pm every day. One bystander pass is
issued per patient. Children under 12 are not allowed in wards.

## ICU visiting
ICU visiting is allowed only from 11:00 am to 11:30 am and 5:00 pm to 5:30 pm, one visitor at a
time. Visiting is not allowed in the ICU outside these hours.
